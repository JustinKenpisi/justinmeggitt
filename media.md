Over the years I have contributed to a wide range of broadcast and digital media, from various documentaries about early Christianity to BBC Radio 4's _In Our Time_. More recently, I've appeared on a number of niche podcasts, talking about everything from religious terrorism and North African slavery to Roman science fiction and the end of the world — and why Bedford is the best place to be when that happens. 

My research and public talks have been featured in publications in the UK and beyond and I regularly support journalists working on religious themes for major media outlets, including _The Economist_, the BBC, _New Scientist_, _The Times_, and _The Guardian_. I've also written for more popular magazines, such as the *Fortean Times* and provided provided specialist advice for drama series.

I am always happy to answer questions about the fields within which I work, or to discuss the study of religion more generally. I care a great deal about the public understanding of religion and the need to foster a critical and informed approach to its various forms today. If you think I can be of any help, please don't hesitate to get in touch.
